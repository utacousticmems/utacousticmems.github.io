Engineering Education and Research Center (EER)

Prof. Hall's office is EER 4.802. The Acoustical MEMS Test Laboratory is on the fourth floor.

Getting there

  1. Park in the San Jacinto Garage.
  2. Leave the garage onto San Jacinto Blvd and walk north.
  3. Cross the back bridge into EER. You arrive on floor 0.
  4. Take the South tower elevators to the fourth floor.

Facilities

Microelectronics Research Center

More than 12,000 sq ft of Class 100 and Class 1000 cleanroom, part of the NSF National Nanotechnology Coordinated Infrastructure (NNCI). Includes an active-materials processing bay for chemical-solution deposition of piezoelectric films, and sputtering systems for aluminum nitride.

Acoustical MEMS Test Laboratory (EER)

Four laser Doppler vibrometers, white-light interferometry, a 6-axis precision fiber aligner, fiber-coupled tunable lasers and photodetectors, 3-axis scanning ultrasound tanks, electromechanical shakers for IMU testing, network analyzers for dielectric characterization, and multi-core workstations running COMSOL, ANSYS and MATLAB.

Center for Nano and Molecular Science (CNM)

Shared characterization tools on campus, including X-ray diffraction and crystallography of piezoelectric materials.

Texas Acoustics

Acoustics research at UT Austin spans five colleges, with twelve acoustics courses in the Cockrell School of Engineering.
