The lab you would work in

Devices are fabricated in the Microelectronics Research Center cleanroom (over 12,000 sq ft of Class 100 and 1000 space, part of the NSF NNCI) and tested in our Acoustical MEMS Test Laboratory in EER. More on facilities.

Earlier work

Projects that shaped the group and several of its alumni.

Fly-inspired hearing

Microphones modeled on the ears of Ormia ochracea. NPR, 2014

Optically read seismometers

Grating-based optical sensors commercialized by Silicon Audio, tested on glaciers in Alaska and Greenland and competing for a mission to Europa. Anchorage Daily News, 2017

Infrasound

Microphones for 0.01 to 20 Hz, the range of earthquakes, volcanoes and explosions, including nuclear test monitoring for treaty verification.

Hypersonic flow sensing

Piezoelectric pressure and shear-stress sensors tested in a Mach 6 facility.

Elastic-wave telemetry

Sending data through vibrations in steel well casing from deep underground storage sites, where radio does not reach.

Capacitive ultrasound

Rotational CMUTs and broadband capacitive microphones with more than 200 kHz bandwidth.
